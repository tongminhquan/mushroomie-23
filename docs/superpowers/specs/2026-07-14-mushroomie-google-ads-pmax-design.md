# Mushroomie Google Ads Performance Max Design

## Objective

Create a Performance Max campaign that prioritizes completed purchases on
`https://mushroomie.io.vn/`. The campaign must not optimize primarily for phone
calls. Existing Merchant Center products remain eligible for Shopping formats.

## Scope

- Campaign type: Performance Max.
- Primary conversion: website purchase.
- Secondary interactions: product views, add to cart, checkout start, and calls;
  secondary actions must not replace purchase as the bidding objective.
- Geographic targeting: Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai.
- Language: Vietnamese.
- Landing page: `https://mushroomie.io.vn/` with relevant product and category
  sitelinks where the interface permits.

## Budget And Schedule

- Daily budget: 30,000 VND.
- Duration: 10 days.
- Planned maximum media budget: 300,000 VND, subject to Google Ads billing and
  daily-spend behavior shown in the final review screen.
- Do not publish until the final review confirms the daily budget, locations,
  purchase objective, campaign dates, and billing account.

## Audience Signals

Use audience signals as guidance rather than hard audience restrictions:

- Handmade accessories and personalized gifts.
- Bracelets, charms, necklaces, keychains, and custom accessories.
- Gift shoppers and young fashion/accessory shoppers.
- Relevant search themes in Vietnamese, including `vong tay handmade`,
  `vong tay custom`, `charm`, `moc khoa handmade`, and `qua tang ca nhan hoa`.

## Asset Group

The asset group should consistently present Mushroomie as a youthful,
handmade, personalized accessory brand. Copy must be natural Vietnamese,
avoid unverifiable claims, and stay within Google Ads character limits.

Required assets:

- At least 5 short headlines, including the 3 required headlines.
- At least 2 long headlines.
- At least 3 descriptions.
- At least 1 landscape image, 1 square image, and 1 square logo.
- Business name: Mushroomie, unless Merchant Center policy forces the linked
  account name and the mismatch must be resolved before publishing.
- Sitelinks for products, custom bracelets, news/gift guidance, and cart or
  contact pages when valid landing routes are available.

## Measurement And Bidding

- Verify that the purchase conversion is available and marked as the primary
  campaign objective before selecting an automated bidding strategy.
- Prefer Maximize conversions for the initial low-budget learning period.
- Do not set an arbitrary target ROAS without reliable historical purchase
  conversion value data.
- Preserve UTM or automatic tagging settings if already configured.

## Safety And Error Handling

- Resolve the current Google Ads ad-blocker warning before entering assets.
- Do not enter or expose payment credentials, OTPs, passwords, or recovery
  information.
- Stop for user confirmation before the final action that publishes the
  campaign or activates billing/spend.
- If the linked Merchant Center name remains `Hago Tree`, report the mismatch
  instead of silently publishing an ad under the wrong business identity.
- If purchase tracking is absent or inactive, stop before bidding setup and
  report the exact missing conversion requirement.

## Verification

Before handoff, verify the review screen shows:

1. Website purchase as the primary optimization objective.
2. Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai as the targeted locations.
3. 30,000 VND daily budget and a 10-day schedule.
4. Valid required text, image, logo, and Merchant Center assets.
5. No unresolved policy, billing, ad-blocker, or conversion-tracking blockers.
6. The campaign remains unpublished until the user gives final confirmation.
