## Quy tac

- Ghi URL nguon cu the sau khi link da ton tai va kiem tra duoc.

- Moi outreach can co chu so huu/nguoi phu trach va bang chung lien quan that.
- Kiem tra link mat, redirect, anchor va NAP moi thang; khong tu dong tao link.
