# B30 Local Authority Operations Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook grows truthful local relevance and prominence for Mushroomie through a complete Google Business Profile, ethical customer reviews, consistent NAP citations and legitimate local relationships. It does not treat an operational checklist as ranking proof.

Organic and Local Pack are measured separately. The B30 parent goal is complete only after the approved scorecard independently proves the correct owner URL at organic position 1 for all 30 exact queries in three consecutive weekly observations, each 6–8 days apart.

## Non-negotiable policy labels

- **no review gating** — every satisfaction level receives the same neutral invitation.
- **no duplicate profile** — audit and claim the existing entity before considering creation.
- **no bulk backlinks** — reject mass directory packages, link farms and unrelated placements.
- **no PBN** — reject private blog networks and manufactured authority.
- **no paid reviews** — do not buy, exchange discounts for, gate or fabricate reviews.

External mutation approval is required immediately before save, send or publish. Read-only inspection does not authorize a later write. Capture the public before value, proposed truthful value, truth source and rollback value before requesting approval.

## Evidence handling

1. Copy `docs/seo-local-b30/authority-evidence-template.md` to storage outside Git.
2. Use the actual observation date in ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
3. Fill every finalized cell; use `not publicly exposed` or `not applicable` where truthful.
4. Keep authenticated screenshots, ownership status details, verification material, cookies, personal data and private exports outside Git.
5. Never print or store a service-account key, token, session value or verification code in the tracker.

## Read-only public baseline

1. Search the exact business name, canonical phone and canonical address while logged out.
2. Inspect Google Search and Maps public results for name, address/service-area mode, phone, website, hours, category, coordinates, rating and review count.
3. Record similar-name candidates separately. Do not assume a candidate belongs to Mushroomie unless phone/address evidence matches.
4. Open the four seeded owned-profile URLs while logged out and record only publicly exposed NAP and link data.
5. Classify each field as `correct`, `incomplete`, `incorrect`, `needs-business-decision` or `duplicate-risk`.
6. Do not suggest edits, create a profile, press Verify or press Save during this phase.

## Google Business Profile corrections

1. Resolve ownership and duplicate risk before editing fields.
2. The business owner completes any Google verification directly; do not handle verification codes or identity evidence.
3. Apply only fields backed by canonical truth or an explicit business decision.
4. Keep the display name `Mushroomie`; do not append locations or keywords.
5. Recheck the public profile logged out after propagation.
6. If Google rejects or suspends a change, stop and investigate; do not create a replacement profile to bypass enforcement.

## Ethical review invitation

Only real customers after a completed transaction may receive an invitation. Every satisfaction level receives the same review link and opt-out. Never condition a gift, discount, voucher or support outcome on leaving a review.

Before enabling any invitation:

1. Confirm the order is completed and the recipient has not opted out.
2. Obtain the official Google review link from the verified profile; never guess or synthesize it.
3. Keep website product reviews separate from Google reviews.
4. Send one neutral invitation through the approved customer communication flow.
5. Record only aggregate delivery status; do not put order identifiers or customer content in this Git tracker.
6. Respond weekly without exposing order or customer data. Escalate abuse or policy issues rather than arguing publicly.

## Citation tracker workflow

The seeded rows in `docs/seo-local-b30/citation-tracker.csv` are an audit queue, not proof of live citations. Blank NAP cells mean not yet audited publicly.

Allowed `verification_status` progression:

- `pending_live_check`: not audited in the current cycle.
- `verified_live`: public page and relevant NAP/link were observed.
- `not_exposed`: platform does not expose the field publicly.
- `mismatch_pending_approval`: a truthful correction is proposed but not approved.
- `unavailable`: public URL is unavailable or cannot be verified.

For each platform:

1. Open the canonical public URL logged out.
2. Record exact public name, address, phone, website, target URL and link attribute when observable.
3. Set `last_checked` only when the inspection actually occurred.
4. Do not mark `verified_live` based on HTTP 200 alone or a JavaScript shell with no visible field evidence.
5. Request approval for one platform at a time before changing it; preserve the handle unless a verified correction requires otherwise.

## Legitimate local authority

Outreach must originate from a real relationship with a Đồng Nai/Biên Hòa organization, event, creator, supplier, school/club or press audience relevant to handmade accessories. Record why the relationship is editorially legitimate and the actual target page. Reject unrelated directories, reciprocal link farms, paid bulk placements and generic profile spam.

A mention is not counted until its public URL, relationship, target URL and live status have been checked. Use natural brand or contextual anchors; do not force exact-match anchors across placements.

## Cadence

Weekly: new/recent review count, unanswered reviews, GBP warnings/suspensions, profile edits.

Monthly: NAP diff, citation live status, broken/redirected target URLs, new legitimate mentions.

Quarterly: category/services/photos accuracy, duplicate profile search, competitor relevance/prominence delta.

Rollback: restore recorded prior field; do not create a replacement profile to bypass a suspension.

## Rollback procedure

1. Stop the batch after the first unexpected rejection, suspension, visibility loss or identity mismatch.
2. Restore only the recorded prior value for the affected field when the platform permits it.
3. Preserve before/after timestamps outside Git and note the public propagation result.
4. Never create a replacement profile, redirect customers to a fake location or delete a verified entity to evade a suspension.
5. Re-run public NAP and duplicate checks before resuming another platform.

## Ranking correlation and success boundary

Record authority changes beside weekly organic and Local Pack observations, but describe correlation only. Do not claim that a citation, review or profile edit caused a ranking change without controlled evidence.

If any exact query lacks position 1 or three valid observations, prioritize: indexation, owner/intent conflict, snippet/CTR, internal/content support, then legitimate authority gaps. Keep the B30 goal active until the measurement contract is fully satisfied.
